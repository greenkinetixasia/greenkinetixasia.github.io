Frequently Asked Questions About EC Cabinet Fans

What is an EC cabinet fan?

An EC cabinet fan combines an electronically commutated motor-driven centrifugal fan with a protective cabinet for ducted supply or exhaust ventilation.

What is the difference between CP-COPRA and CDP?

CP-COPRA uses a backward-curved plug fan arrangement and offers airflow up to 20,000 m³/h. CDP uses a double-inlet forward-curved arrangement and offers airflow up to 5,500 m³/h.

Can EC cabinet fans connect to a BMS?

CP-COPRA supports 0–10 VDC and Modbus RS485 controls as listed for this product range. CDP is listed with a 0–10 V control signal. Confirm the specific fan model and interface before integration.

What information is needed for a cabinet fan quotation?

Provide the airflow, static pressure, power supply, installation arrangement, operating conditions, acoustic requirements and control preferences.
